SECTION 1 • HIGHEST IMPORTANCE

In-Vehicle & Engine Start Inspection

* AUTOMATIC FAILURE IF NOT PERFORMED CORRECTLY: You MUST perform the Air Brake Test in the exact sequence and verbalize all pressure cut-offs and audible/visual warnings.

* Air Brake Check (POOPSLAB)

Automatic Failure Item
P
P - Purge: With engine running, build pressure. Governor cuts OUT between 120 – 140 PSI with a clear purge sneeze.
O
O - Off: Turn the engine OFF.
O
O - On (Accessories): Turn key to ON/Accessories position (do NOT start engine).
P
P - Push Pedal: Press and hold the brake pedal firmly for 60 seconds. Must NOT lose more than 3 PSI.
S
S - Settle: Let gauges settle — confirm no less than 3 PSI was lost. Hold and observe gauges stabilize.
L
L - Low Air Warning: Fan/pump brake pedal down. Warning buzzer and red light MUST activate at or above 60 PSI.
A
A - Alarm Pop-Out: Continue fanning brake pedal. Between 20 – 40 PSI, the yellow parking brake and red trailer valves MUST pop out automatically.
B
B - Build-Up: Start engine. Pressure builds back up. Governor cuts OUT between 120 – 140 PSI — truck is ready to roll.
EXACT VERBAL SCRIPT TO SAY TO EXAMINER (POOPSLAB):

"Examiner, I am now conducting my Air Brake Check using the POOPSLAB sequence. First — Purge: With engine running, my air pressure built to between 120 and 140 PSI and the governor cut out with a purge sneeze. Next — Off: I turn the engine off. On: I turn the key to accessories. Push: I apply the foot brake firmly and hold for 60 seconds — my gauges show I did not lose more than 3 PSI. Settle: Gauges have settled and confirmed no leak. Low Air Warning: I fan the brakes down — at 60 PSI or above, my red warning light and audible buzzer activated. Alarm Pop-Out: Continuing to fan — between 20 and 40 PSI, my yellow parking brake valve and red trailer valve popped out into emergency set position. Build-Up: I start the engine, pressure builds back to 120 to 140 PSI, and the governor cut-out is confirmed. My air brakes are functioning correctly."

Parking & Trailer Brake Check (Tug Test)

Step 1Parking brake engaged (make sure yellow button is OUT)
Step 2Put truck in Drive
Step 3Remove foot from service brake
Step 4Push gently on gas (tugging forward) → "My parking brakes are working correctly because my truck did not move."
Step 5Back into Neutral
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"My parking brake is engaged — the yellow button is out. I place the truck in drive and gently apply the gas, tugging forward against the brake. My truck did not move, confirming my parking brakes are working correctly."

Service Brake Check (5 MPH Test)

Step 1Release parking brake — push yellow button IN
Step 2Put truck in Drive
Step 3Pull forward 20–30 feet at 5 MPH
Step 4Gently stop — steering wheel must NOT shift left or right
Step 5"My service brakes are working correctly."
Step 6Back into Neutral → Pull parking brake back out
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"I release the parking brake and pull forward 20 to 30 feet at approximately 5 miles per hour. I press the service brake firmly. The vehicle came to a complete, smooth stop. The steering wheel did not shift left or right — my service brakes are working correctly."

Emergency Equipment (Fuses, Extinguisher, Triangles)

Windshield & Traffic Monitoring Devices (Clean, Obstructions, Flaws, Clean & Adjusted)

Look For: Clean glass, no obstructions/stickers in line of sight, no cracks or spidering, mirrors securely mounted and adjusted for driver visibility.

VERBAL SCRIPT:

"My windshield is clean, free of obstructions, no illegal stickers, and has no cracks, stars, or chips. All traffic monitoring mirrors (flat and convex) are securely mounted, clean, not cracked or broken, and properly adjusted to my driving view."

Wipers & Washers (Secure, Damage, Work / Squirt)

Note from Sheet: "secure, damage, work/squirt"

VERBAL SCRIPT:

"My wiper arms and blades are securely attached with strong tension. The rubber blades are not dry-rotted, torn, or damaged. When activated, the washer fluid squirts properly across the windshield, and the wipers operate smoothly without chattering or streaking."

Heater & Defroster Working

Action: Turn switch to defrost, feel air blowing against windshield. Switch to heat, feel warm air on feet.

VERBAL SCRIPT:

"I am testing my heater and defroster. I feel strong airflow coming from the top windshield defroster vents. I switch to floor heat and feel warm airflow at the lower floor vents. Both blowers are functioning properly."

Horn(s) - City & Air Horns

Note: You must test BOTH the electric (City) horn and the pneumatic (Air) horn!

⚠️ IMPORTANT — AIR HORN PSI REQUIREMENT:

The Air Horn will NOT work until air pressure reaches at least 90 PSI. Make sure pressure is built up before testing it!

VERBAL SCRIPT:

"I sound my City electric horn [Honk] - it works loud and clear. I pull the lanyard to sound my Air horn [Honk] - it sounds properly with full air pressure."

"I will now ask the examiner to assist in checking external lights. Front of Vehicle & Engine Area

Examiner Checklist: Proper color (Amber in front, clear headlights), clean, not missing, not broken or cracked.

"All front lenses are clean, proper color (clear headlights, amber clearance lights), none are missing, cracked, or broken."

Fluid Levels (Coolant, Oil, Power Steering)

Coolant Between add & full mark / sight glass visible
Engine Oil Dipstick pulled, wiped, level between ADD & FULL
Power Steering Reservoir cap / dipstick above minimum operating range
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"Coolant reservoir is securely mounted, level is above the cold fill mark with no leaks. Engine oil dipstick pulled, wiped clean, reinserted; oil level is between minimum and maximum operating marks. Power steering reservoir is securely mounted and level is within safe operating range."

Fluid & Air Leaks (Puddles, Eng/Trans, Air)

Key Points: Look on ground underneath vehicle for fresh puddles of oil, coolant, transmission fluid, or fuel. Listen for hissing air leaks.

VERBAL SCRIPT:

"Looking underneath the engine, transmission, and front axle: there are no puddles, wet spots, or active fluid leaks. I do not hear any audible air hissing or air leaks."

Steering System (Box, Hoses, Links/Arms/Rods/Joints, Hardware & Cotter Pin)

Circled Note: Cotter Pin
Box: Securely mounted to frame, no missing bolts, no fluid leaks.
Hoses: Power steering pressure and return lines not cracked, leaking, or worn.
Links / Arms / Rods / Joints / Socket: Pitman arm, drag link, steering knuckle, tie rod not cracked, bent, loose, or worn.
Hardware: All bolts, castle nuts, and COTTER PINS present and secure.
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"My steering gear box is securely mounted to the truck frame, all mounting bolts are tight, and there are no power steering fluid leaks. Steering hoses are not cracked, chafed, or leaking. The pitman arm, drag link, steering arm, and tie rod have no excessive play, no cracks or bends. All castle nuts are tight and all COTTER PINS are present and locked."

SECTION 4 • TIRES, SUSPENSION & WHEEL END

Front Wheel End, Suspension & Brakes

Tires (ICD Rule: Inflation, Condition, Depth 4/32" & 2/32")

Circled Note: 4/32, 2/32
I
Inflation: Measured with tire air gauge at accessible valve stem. (Checked when cold, properly inflated to tire spec, valve cap present).
C
Condition: Even wear, NO cuts, exposed cords/wires, NO bumps or bulges in sidewall or tread.
D
Depth: Minimum 4/32" on FRONT STEER tires; minimum 2/32" on drive/trailer tires.
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"Inspecting tires using I-C-D. I for Inflation: air pressure checked with a tire gauge at the valve stem, properly inflated to manufacturer PSI; valve cap is not missing, broken, or damaged. C for Condition: tread is evenly worn, sidewalls have no cuts, no exposed cords or wires, and no bumps or bulges. D for Depth: tread depth is verified with a gauge to have at least 4/32 inch minimum depth on steer tires, and 2/32 inch on all other tires. Steer tires are virgin rubber, not recaps."

Rims, Lug Nuts & Studs

Rims: No cracks, dents, bends, illegal welds, or elongated bolt holes.
Lug Nuts: All present, none missing; check for rust trails on steel rims or shiny metal threads on aluminum wheels (evidence of looseness).
Studs: None missing, loose, stripped, or broken.
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"The wheel rims have no cracks, dents, bends, or unauthorized repair welds, and bolt holes are not elongated. All lug nuts are present and tight—there are no rust streaks or shiny threads which would indicate looseness. Wheel studs are not missing, loose, or broken."

Springs, Mounts, U-Bolts, Shocks (+ Level Check)

Vehicle Stance: Truck sits level side-to-side and front-to-back (not leaning).
Springs: Leaf springs properly aligned, not shifted, scissored, cracked, or broken.
Mounts: Spring hangers, shackles, and bushings secure, not broken or worn.
U-Bolts: Both U-bolts securely clamping axle, nuts tight, not broken.
Shock Absorbers: Securely mounted top and bottom, no dents, no hydraulic fluid leaks.
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"Looking at vehicle stance, the truck sits level side-to-side and front-to-back. The leaf springs are secure, none shifted, cracked, missing, or broken. Spring mounts and hangers are not cracked, broken, or missing hardware. U-bolts are tight and undamaged with no missing nuts. The shock absorber is securely mounted top and bottom with no damage or hydraulic oil leaks."

Brake Lines, Hoses, Leaks & Brake Contaminates

Brake Lines / Hoses: Supply air lines secure, no audible leaks, not cracked, frayed, or worn; fittings tight.
Brake Contaminates: Brake shoes/pads and brake drums/rotors are free from oil, grease, brake fluid, or debris; linings not worn dangerously thin.
VERBAL SCRIPT:

"Air brake lines and hoses are securely mounted, no audible air leaks, not cracked, frayed, or worn, and all fittings are secure. Inspecting brake friction surfaces: brake drums and linings are clean, free of oil, grease, or contaminants, and linings have sufficient friction material thickness."

🧠 CDL Pre-Trip Inspection Mnemonics

P - O - O - P - S - L - A - B

Air Brake Check Sequence (POOPSLAB)

P = Purge: Build pressure — governor cuts OUT between 120–140 PSI with a purge sneeze.
O = Off: Turn engine OFF.
O = On (Accessories): Turn key to ON/Accessories position.
P = Push Pedal: Hold foot brake firmly for 60 seconds. Max 3 PSI drop.
S = Settle: Let gauges settle — confirm no more than 3 PSI lost.
L = Low Air Warning: Fan brakes down. Light & buzzer must sound at or above 60 PSI.
A = Alarm Pop-Out: Continue fanning. Valves pop out automatically between 20–40 PSI.
B = Build-Up: Start engine. Pressure builds back to 120–140 PSI. Governor cuts out — truck is ready.
I - C - D

Tire Inspection Rule

I = Inflation: Check cold pressure with air gauge at accessible valve stem; valve cap present.
C = Condition: Even wear, no cuts, no exposed wires/cords, no bumps or bulges in tread or sidewall.
D = Depth: Minimum 4/32" on steer tires; minimum 2/32" on all drive and trailer tires.
P - M - S

Mounting Universal Standard

Every single bracket, tank, steering box, suspension mount, and mirror must be verbalized as:

"Properly Mounted and Secure, with no missing bolts or hardware."
C - D - L

Damage Universal Standard

Check every hose, pipe, reservoir, and metal component for:

"Not Cracked, Not Damaged, and Not Leaking."
L - R - 4 - C - H - L

Lights Sequence from Sheet

L: Left Turn Signal
R: Right Turn Signal
4: 4-Way Emergency Hazards
C: Clearance / Marker Lights
H / L: Headlights (High Beam & Low Beam)
F - E - T

Emergency Equipment

F = Fuses: Spare electrical fuses of every rating.
E = Extinguisher: Fully charged (gauge green), pin secured, rated 10 B:C or 5 B:C.
T = Triangles: 3 red reflective warning triangles in good condition.
